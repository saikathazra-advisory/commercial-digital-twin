# Changelog

 

## Version 2.0.0

 

Commercial Digital Twin 2.0 MVP Baseline

 

### Features

 

- Predefined scenario simulation

- Custom scenario simulation

- Market opportunity analysis

- Product recommendation engine

- Priority account engine

- Pricing simulation

- Bundle strategy simulation

- Revenue model

- Capacity and risk assessment

- Scenario comparison

- Model evidence tab

- CSV exports

 

### Validation

 

- Account-level ACV reconciles to scenario Projected New ACV

- Account win probabilities reconcile to Expected Customers Won

- Product TAM reconciles to Segment TAM

- Priority Account counts reconcile to Focus Accounts

 

### Notes

 

- All datasets are synthetic.

- No real company or customer data is included.

- Version 2.0.0 represents the locked MVP baseline.
